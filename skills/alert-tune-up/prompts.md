# Prompts

## Start an audit

- Run an alert tune-up for my client **Acme** for the last 14 days.
- Which alerts fired the most last month across my whole account, and which can I turn off?
- My on-call channel is flooded with Domotz alerts from **the Main Street site**. Audit them.
- Audit alerts on collector **Warehouse** for 1–30 September.

## During the audit

- Why is **B2** safe? Show me the alerts.
- What would **B3** look like with a threshold of 100 ms instead?
- Which devices does rule **Device Status** cover on this collector?
- Move **L3** to the proposed changes; that device isn't important to us.

## Approve and apply

- Approve B1.
- Approve B1 and B3, skip B2.
- Approve all.
- Undo B1.

## Follow up

- Here is last month's report: *(paste it)*. Compare it with this month.
- Summarise what we changed today in three lines for my ticket.
