# Account management

Domotz know-how for your AI assistant, loaded on its own when a question needs it.

**What it covers:** How to manage a Domotz account: users, groups and Access Sets, two-factor and SAML single sign-on, invoices and payment, changing or cancelling the subscription, account ownership and deletion, API keys, and connecting AI clients to Domotz through MCP.

**Changes it can make:** None: it reads the account basics (`get_account_info`) and gives you the steps, or hands off to Domotz support.

The facts come from the Domotz Help Center and Domotz's own teams, and are the same ones the AI
assistant inside the Domotz app uses. When something here disagrees with
[help.domotz.com](https://help.domotz.com), the Help Center wins: tell us at support@domotz.com.

**Ask, for example:**
- How do I give a new technician read-only access to one client?
- How do I connect Copilot Studio to Domotz?
- How do I turn on SSO with Okta?
