# Alerting

Domotz know-how for your AI assistant, loaded on its own when a question needs it.

**What it covers:** How Domotz alerting works and how to set it up: Alert Rules and the older Shared and Personal Alerts, contact channels (email, push, Microsoft Teams, Slack, webhooks), Collector alerts, the internet going down, snoozing, PSA ticketing (Autotask, ConnectWise, HaloPSA and others) and documentation sync (IT Glue and others). It also walks through why an alert, a push notification or a PSA ticket did not arrive.

**Changes it can make:** It reads alert rules, bindings, channels and alert history. With your approval of each change: create and update rules, bind and unbind them, snooze a collector, map a collector to a PSA or documentation customer, resolve an alert you name (resolving cannot be undone).

The facts come from the Domotz Help Center and Domotz's own teams, and are the same ones the AI
assistant inside the Domotz app uses. When something here disagrees with
[help.domotz.com](https://help.domotz.com), the Help Center wins: tell us at support@domotz.com.

**Ask, for example:**
- How do I get a Teams message when the internet goes down at the Pisa office?
- Why didn't I get a push notification when the NAS went offline last night?
- Tickets stopped arriving in ConnectWise: what should I check?
