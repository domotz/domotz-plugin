# Collector setup

Domotz know-how for your AI assistant, loaded on its own when a question needs it.

**What it covers:** Where and how to install a Collector (Windows, Linux, Docker, virtual machines, NAS, the Domotz Box), sizing, activation, the firewall hosts it needs, why one is offline, updates, restarts, moving it to new hardware and removing it.

**Changes it can make:** It reads collector status, health, version and uptime. With your approval: rename a collector, set its time zone and address, change its settings, snooze its alerts. Installing, migrating, restarting and deleting are steps it gives you.

The facts come from the Domotz Help Center and Domotz's own teams, and are the same ones the AI
assistant inside the Domotz app uses. When something here disagrees with
[help.domotz.com](https://help.domotz.com), the Help Center wins: tell us at support@domotz.com.

**Ask, for example:**
- What hardware should I use for a collector at a 400-device site?
- The collector at Acme has been offline since this morning: what do I check?
- How do I move the collector to a new server without losing history?
