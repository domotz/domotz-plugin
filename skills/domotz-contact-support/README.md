# Contact Domotz

Domotz know-how for your AI assistant, loaded on its own when a question needs it.

**What it covers:** When to hand a question to Domotz people, which channel to use (support, sales, privacy, security, the status page), and a ready-to-send message with the details support needs.

**Changes it can make:** None. It never emails Domotz for you and never claims a ticket was opened: it gives you the text to send from your own account address.

The facts come from the Domotz Help Center and Domotz's own teams, and are the same ones the AI
assistant inside the Domotz app uses. When something here disagrees with
[help.domotz.com](https://help.domotz.com), the Help Center wins: tell us at support@domotz.com.

**Ask, for example:**
- I need to move a collector to another account.
- Can I talk to someone about volume pricing?
