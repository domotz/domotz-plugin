# Reach Domotz support or sales

## When to hand off

- The user asks for a person, support, a ticket, sales, a quote, a demo, a discount or a trial extension.
- The task needs Domotz staff: transferring the Account Owner role, changing a username, enabling a feature on the account (for example Alert Rules on an older account, or RBAC on a Company Structure account), moving a Collector to another account, deleting the account, billing disputes.
- You tried and cannot do it with your tools, or the product does not support it. Say so in one sentence, then hand off. Do not hand off what you can do yourself.

## Which channel

| Need | Channel |
|---|---|
| A technical problem (Collector, devices, alerts, integrations), account, billing, feature requests | support@domotz.com |
| Quotes, the per-device plan, volume or annual pricing, demos | "Contact Sales" on https://www.domotz.com/contact-us.php (books a meeting), or sales@domotz.com |
| Privacy, the DPA, deleting the account's data | privacy@domotz.com |
| A security vulnerability | it-security@domotz.com |
| Is Domotz itself down? | https://status.domotz.com |

- Support hours: European and US working hours, Monday to Friday. The published support policy expects an email reply within 1 business day.
- Support acts only on email sent from the address of an active Domotz user of the account.

## What to give the user

Offer a short message they can paste, built only from what you know in this conversation:
- the account email (the user's own);
- the Collector and site name, and the device name and IP address;
- what they wanted, what happened, the exact error text, and since when;
- what was already tried, including what you checked.

If support needs to look inside a Collector, the user can share it: Collector page, then Support, then the pen icon next to "Support Team", then "Yes, share it". It is revoked the same way with "Yes, revoke it".

## Never

- Never send the user to a support chat: support is reached by email.
- Never say that someone will contact the user, and never give a response time other than the published one above.
- Never email Domotz for the user, even if you have an email tool: support acts only on email from the user's own account address, so give them the text to send. Never claim to have opened a ticket or sent a message.
- Never ask for, or repeat, passwords, API keys or card numbers.
