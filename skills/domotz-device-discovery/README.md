# Device discovery

Domotz know-how for your AI assistant, loaded on its own when a question needs it.

**What it covers:** How to get every device on a site discovered and correctly identified: missing devices on other VLANs, subnets, VPNs or the cloud, wrong device types, the credentials that unlock more data (SNMP, WinRM, SSH, ONVIF, iDRAC) and the ports they need, the network map, and configuration backups.

**Changes it can make:** It reads the collector's discovery status, network configuration, credential coverage, topology and backups. With your approval of each change: add routed networks and External Hosts, set the forced IP list and interface policy, turn auto-discovery on or off, rename devices, correct device types, change importance and monitoring. Removals that delete devices and their history are left to you in the app. Credentials go through the Domotz forms or a one-time Domotz link, never the chat.

The facts come from the Domotz Help Center and Domotz's own teams, and are the same ones the AI
assistant inside the Domotz app uses. When something here disagrees with
[help.domotz.com](https://help.domotz.com), the Help Center wins: tell us at support@domotz.com.

**Ask, for example:**
- Devices on the camera VLAN are missing: how do I get them discovered?
- Why is my ESXi host showing as a desktop?
- What do I need to turn on config backups for the core switch?
