# Pricing and plans

Domotz know-how for your AI assistant, loaded on its own when a question needs it.

**What it covers:** The two pricing models (per location and per device) and how to tell which one an account is on, the free trial, billing terms, the Domotz Box, distributors and marketplaces. It states only the published starting price and sends quotes and volume pricing to Domotz sales.

**Changes it can make:** None. It reads the collectors' monitoring mode to tell which model the account is on.

The facts come from the Domotz Help Center and Domotz's own teams, and are the same ones the AI
assistant inside the Domotz app uses. When something here disagrees with
[help.domotz.com](https://help.domotz.com), the Help Center wins: tell us at support@domotz.com.

**Ask, for example:**
- What would Domotz cost for 12 sites?
- Am I on per-device or per-location pricing?
- How long is the free trial?
