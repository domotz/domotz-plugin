# Security and compliance

Domotz know-how for your AI assistant, loaded on its own when a question needs it.

**What it covers:** Domotz's security and privacy facts for your own answers and your customers' vendor questionnaires: certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II), hosting and data residency, encryption, access control, logging, incident response, privacy and deletion, subprocessors, and how Domotz's AI features and the AI clients connected through MCP handle data.

**Changes it can make:** None: read-only facts. Documents such as the SOC 2 report are on https://trust.domotz.com, some on request under NDA.

The facts come from the Domotz Help Center and Domotz's own teams, and are the same ones the AI
assistant inside the Domotz app uses. When something here disagrees with
[help.domotz.com](https://help.domotz.com), the Help Center wins: tell us at support@domotz.com.

**Ask, for example:**
- Where is my clients' data stored, and is it encrypted?
- Help me answer this vendor security questionnaire for Domotz.
- Does Domotz use our data to train AI models?
